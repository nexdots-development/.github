## Who we are

NexDots is a software development studio based in Phnom Penh, Cambodia, with a team of 10+ engineers. We partner with startups and businesses to plan, design, build and run digital products.

## What we do

- **Mobile apps**: cross-platform with Flutter, plus native iOS and Android
- **Web apps & websites**: React, Next.js and Vue frontends backed by Node.js APIs
- **UI/UX design**: product design, prototyping and design systems
- **Cloud, DevOps & AI integration**: containerized deployments, CI/CD and AI-powered features
